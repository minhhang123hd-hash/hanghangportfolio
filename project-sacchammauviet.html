Overview

Context: a workshop concept celebrating traditional Vietnamese culture, built from the ground up. Audience: students and young professionals interested in heritage & craft. My role: Project Leader — originated the concept and owned it end-to-end, directing 29 members across Event, Communications, and External Relations.

2

The challenge

Take a cultural concept from zero to a fully ticketed, sold-out live event in just three weeks — planning the timeline, coordinating three functional teams, and personally directing every visual touchpoint.

Strategy & execution

Management & flow: built and managed the Gantt chart and project timeline coordinating Event, Communications, and External Relations.

Creative & visual assets: directed the full communications set — brochure, leaflet, invitation, and POSM — as content director for the campaign.

Cross-functional alignment: ran the event-day operation personally, coordinating all three teams in real time.

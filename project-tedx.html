Overview

Context: TEDxFTU is a licensed TEDx event, meaning every asset must meet TED's global brand standard. Audience: students, alumni, and the wider Hanoi TEDx community. My role: led the Multimedia team, owning channel strategy, production timeline, and brand-consistency review across Threads and TikTok.

2

The challenge

Grow the event's social presence from a near-zero base while strictly complying with TEDx's global guidelines — and keep the Multimedia, Event, and Curation teams aligned on one consistent message, on a fixed pre-event timeline.

3

Strategy & execution

Management & flow: built the content production timeline, balanced workload across the Multimedia team, and ran the weekly sync between Multimedia, Event, and Curation to keep messaging on-brand.

Creative & visual assets: directed the Threads/TikTok content roadmap and format mix.

Cross-functional alignment: acted as the quality-control bridge, reviewing every asset against TEDx's global brand guidelines before publishing.

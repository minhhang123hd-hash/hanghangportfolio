Overview

Context: Sky Pacific Education is a Vietnamese study-abroad consultancy focused on South Korea programmes. Audience: Vietnamese students & parents evaluating study-abroad options. My role: Marketing Lead — led a 10-person team through a 6-month integrated marketing campaign.
